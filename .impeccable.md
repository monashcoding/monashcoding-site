# Design Context

## Users
Monash students (mostly IT/CS, first year through grad) landing on monashcoding.com from
Instagram, O Week QR codes, Discord and search. They want to know what MAC is, join, find
events, and, increasingly, get to the tools MAC builds (MonMap, Jobs, MAC Study, Resources,
Hackathons, Member Pricing, event sites). Committee members use the same site as the front door
to their internal tools. Sponsors read it to judge whether MAC is worth partnering with.

## Brand Personality
Confident, student-built, a little cheeky. MAC ships real software, and the site should feel
like the work of people who build things rather than a brochure.

The brand system is fixed: charcoal `#252525`, surfaces `#1E1E1E`/`#1A1A1A`, hairline borders
`white/10`, one accent `#FFE330` (always with `#252525` text on it), indigo `#5757D3` only for
mascots and illustration. Poppins only (400-900); brand consistency overrides the two-font
default. Dark only. No drop shadows: depth comes from surface lightness steps, hairlines and
hover lifts. `rounded-md` buttons, `rounded-lg` cards, `rounded-full` logo. All-caps
`font-semibold` micro-labels at `tracking-[0.08em]`-`[0.2em]`. Giant clamp() `font-extrabold`
headlines.

Copy rules: no em dashes, proper capitalisation in nav and links, no invented statistics.

## Aesthetic Direction
Hackathon-landing energy: loud type, yellow used like a highlighter, hairline structure.
Motion vocabulary: `[0.22,1,0.36,1]` for reveals and hovers, `[0.76,0,0.24,1]` for overlays and
wipes. Signature moves: yellow bar text-reveal wipes, yellow-duplicate vertical slide on link
hover, CTA wipe plus shimmer, `y:-2` hover lifts, yellow click sparks, the cursor ribbon.

## Design Principles
1. Yellow is scarce: it marks the action, the active item and the newest thing, never decoration.
2. Show the work: MAC's apps are presented as living miniatures in MAC's own visual language,
   not as screenshots of other people's UI chrome.
3. One account, one front door: the launcher is the same everywhere and reflects who is signed in.
4. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (no autoplay, crossfades instead of slides).
5. Every auto-advancing thing can be paused.
